## Headline conclusion

First-pass tier: **Limited risk / transparency (Article 50(1))** — Whizbiz converses directly with a
user but makes no decision about a person, so it sits outside Article 5 and every Annex III category.
Two gaps matter most: no in-product AI disclosure, and no AI-literacy/limitations material for users.
Neither requires a redesign. Full reasoning in [02-risk-tier-classification_EU AI Act.md](02-risk-tier-classification_EU%20AI%20Act.md)
and [04-gap-analysis-remediation_EU AI Act.md](04-gap-analysis-remediation_EU%20AI%20Act.md).

rather than after my self-audit was finished — see the process note at the top of
[06-debrief-exchange-with-ugo_EUAI Act.md](06-debrief-exchange-with-ugo_EUAI%20Act.md) for how that's handled honestly
